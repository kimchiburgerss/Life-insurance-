## Who we are and what we do

We are a private company that helps people recover unclaimed property — mainly
life-insurance and annuity payments — that the State of Nevada is holding for
someone who has died. We are not part of, or endorsed by, the State of Nevada or
any government agency. **You can always claim unclaimed property yourself, for
free, from the Nevada State Treasurer's Office, Unclaimed Property Division, at
claims.nevadaunclaimedproperty.gov.**

## What personal information we collect

| Information | Where it comes from |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Name of the person who died, the property, the holder (e.g. insurer), amounts and dates | The State of Nevada's public unclaimed-property records |
| Information about the person who died and their family (names, relationships, places, dates) | Public records such as obituaries and court records, and licensed data providers |
| Your name, mailing address, email address and phone number | Public records and licensed data providers, used only to reach you about this property; or you, when you contact us |
| Messages you send us, notes of our calls with you, and whether you asked us to stop contacting you | You |
| Documents you upload or mail to us for a claim (for example a death certificate, your photo ID, proof of relationship, affidavits) | You |
| Your signature on our agreement, and the date | You (through our e-signature provider or on paper) |
| The amount the State paid you and your payment of our fee | You, the State, and our payment processor |
| Technical information when you use your private web page: IP address, browser type, times of visits | Your browser (we use it for security only — no advertising or tracking cookies) |

We **never** ask for your Social Security number. If a document shows it, please
cover it before sending. We automatically remove numbers that look like one from anything you type to us.

## Why we use it

- To find the rightful heirs of unclaimed property and to contact you about it — by mail or email
  only. We do not make automated calls or send unsolicited text messages.
- If you choose to work with us: to prepare our agreement, prepare and check the documents for your
  claim, help you file it with the State, and invoice our fee after the State pays you.
- To keep records the law requires, to honour your request not to be contacted, and to protect our
  systems and your information.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. (Nevada residents: you may
still send a request not to sell your covered information to [email]; we will confirm we do not sell it.)

## How we protect it

Contact details and documents are encrypted in our database; access requires a password and a second
factor; every action on your file is recorded in a tamper-resistant log; only the people who need it
can see it.

## How long we keep it

| Situation | What we delete, and when |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| You do not work with us, or we stop the search | Your contact details, messages and documents: 180 days after the file is closed |
| You worked with us | Documents for the claim: 2 years after the file is closed; the agreement and invoices: 7 years (tax and legal records) |
| You asked us not to contact you | We keep a coded (hashed) note of your email or address so we never contact you again — it can't be read back as your details |
