## Questions people often ask

**How did you find me?**
The State of Nevada keeps a public list of unclaimed property. We look for owners on that list who have died. Then we use public records, such as obituaries and court files, to find their family. We do not have access to private records held by the State.

**Is this a scam?**
It is a fair question. You do not have to take our word for anything. You can look up the property yourself at {{claimWebsite}} and claim it from the State for free. We never ask for money up front. We will never ask for your Social Security number by email.

**Do you work for the State?**
No. {{privateCompany}}

**What does it cost?**
Nothing, if you claim it yourself. {{feeSentence}}

**What happens if I do nothing?**
Nothing changes. You can still contact the State yourself whenever you like.

**How do I stop your letters?**
Tell us by phone, email or mail, or visit {{optOutUrl}}. We will add you to our do-not-contact list and will not write to you again.
