## 4. Our services

If you sign, we will:

- explain what the State needs for a claim by an heir of {{ownerName}};
- help you gather the documents, such as a death certificate and proof of your relationship;

We are not lawyers and do not give legal advice. We cannot promise that the State will approve the claim or how much it will pay.
